## 1. Beverage Sales Dashboard

### Objective

Understand how the business performs over time and across beverage brands, and identify which brands and months drive revenue and profit.

### What I built

- Slicers for **Beverage Brand, District and Division** so any view can be filtered instantly
- KPI cards: **Total Units Sold, Average Price per Unit, Total Profit, Total Revenue**
- **Total Revenue by Month** (line chart) to show seasonality
- **Total Units Sold by Date** (daily line chart) to show demand volatility
- **Profit vs Revenue by Brand** (clustered bar chart)
- **Revenue share by Brand** (donut chart)

### Key insights

- **Strong seasonality.** Revenue is flat from January to April (about 0.46-0.48M per month), then climbs sharply from May.
- **Two peaks.** July (0.97M) and December (0.98M, the highest month of the year), with a dip in September-October (0.65M and 0.60M).
- **Second half drives the year.** July-December generates about 4.84M, roughly 59% of annual revenue.
- **Coca-Cola leads** with 1.92M (23.4% of revenue), followed by **Dasani Water** at 1.64M (about 20%). Together they account for about 43% of revenue.
- **Mid-tier brands are close together:** Diet Coke (1.39M), Sprite (1.16M) and Powerade (1.13M). **Fanta is the smallest** at 0.97M (11.8%).

- **Daily demand is volatile**, ranging from near 0 to about 190K units per day with no strong long-term trend, so the monthly view is the better planning lens.

### Business recommendations

- Build inventory and marketing capacity ahead of **May-July** and **November-December**.
- Investigate the **autumn dip** (Sep-Oct) with promotions or product mix changes.
- Protect the top two brands and review pricing/costs for lower-margin brands.
